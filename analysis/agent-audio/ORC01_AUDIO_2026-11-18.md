## Mixer limits

* **Voices:** `AudioConfig.max_voices = 64` on the production path
  (`start_game_audio`), per-`SoundInfo`-channel cap 1
  (`DEFAULT_CHANNEL_VOICES`), `CHANNELFULLPREEMPT`/`NODUPLICATES` honoured by
  `Core::admit_channel`; overflow steals the lowest-priority voice. Orc01
  measured `max_active=11`, `chan_full=402`, `chan_preempt=1454`,
  `deduped=45` — the caps engage, nothing is silently stacked.
* **Ack spacing:** `GameAudioConfig.ack_min_interval_ms = 150` on the
  deterministic sim clock. Orc01 proves a 40 ms second tap is dropped:
  `AUDIO_ACK nhar limited=1 gap_ms=40`.
* **Decoded PCM:** 64 MB ambience-cache ceiling
  (`DEFAULT_AMBIENT_PCM_BUDGET`), reported as `pcm_mb` in
  `GAME_AUDIO_HOOKS`. Orc01 peak **21.5 MB** (cache 2 themes / 20 MB,
  `evicted=0`); the 600 s stress test peaks **58.5 MB** with 12 evictions and
  never crosses the ceiling.
* **Pause:** `GameAudio::set_paused` captures the Music/Ambient bus gains,
  sets both to 0, and restores them on resume. Orc01 asserts both buses are
  exactly 0 while paused and non-zero after resume (`AUDIO_PAUSE` lines), and
  keeps UI/voices audible.
