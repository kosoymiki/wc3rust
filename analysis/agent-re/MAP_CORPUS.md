## 2. Canonical 1v1 selection

Selection filter (all from the row data, no heuristics):

1. `MapFlags.MeleeMap` set;
2. exactly **2** playable players in the `w3i` table;
3. **2** `sloc` start markers, both inside the map and on walkable WPM cells;
4. at least two `ngol`/`ugol` mines, all on walkable cells, one within 1200 units of each start;
5. a `war3map.wpm` grid (required by the runtime scene loader);
6. zero parse errors across `w3e`/`w3i`/`wpm`/`doo`.

**34** maps pass the melee/playability filter (2p: 2 · 4p: 23 · 6p: 6 · 8p: 3), of which **32** are authored by Blizzard Entertainment (2p: 1 · 4p: 22 · 6p: 6 · 8p: 3).
